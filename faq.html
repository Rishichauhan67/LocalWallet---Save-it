About Save Anything

Save Anything is a personal content-saving app that lets you save useful information in one place. You can save text, website links, screenshots or images, and Instagram Reel URLs. Your saved items can be organized using categories, tags, favorites, search, and filters.

Save Anything is not a social media platform or a video downloading service. Instagram Reel URLs are saved as links; the Reel video itself is not downloaded or stored.

You can save four types of content in the MVP: text, website links, images or screenshots, and Instagram Reel URLs. Each saved item can have a title, category, tags, favorite status, and creation date.

Your account uses Firebase Authentication. Saved item information is stored in Cloud Firestore, while uploaded images are stored in Firebase Storage. Each user's saved items are associated with their Firebase user ID.
