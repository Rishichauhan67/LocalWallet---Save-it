Privacy

Your Content. Your Privacy.

Save Anything keeps your saved content connected to your account and uses Firebase services to securely store your data.

Privacy Policy

Save Anything stores the content you choose to save, including text, links, images, Reel URLs, categories, tags and favorites.

Information We Collect

We use your email and Firebase account ID for authentication. Your saved items are stored under your account so your content stays associated with you.

How We Protect Your Data

Firebase Authentication, Firestore and Storage are used to manage your account, saved data and uploaded images. You can edit or permanently delete your saved items.
