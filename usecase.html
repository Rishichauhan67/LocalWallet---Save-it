EXPLORE / USECASES

Save it for Everything

Save Anything is designed to give you one private place to save, organize and manage the content you find useful. Your saved items are connected to your account.

Your Account & Content

Save Anything uses Firebase Authentication to create and manage your account.

Each saved item belongs to the authenticated user who created it. Your saved content is associated with your Firebase user ID.

  • Email/password authentication
  • User-specific saved items
  • Login and logout
  • Only your account should access your content

What We Store

When you save something, the application stores the information required to display and organize that item.

  • Text: title and content
  • Links: URL and optional title
  • Reels: Instagram Reel URL
  • Images: uploaded image and Storage URL
  • Organization: category and tags
  • Date: saved/created timestamp

Images are stored in Firebase Storage, while item information is stored in Cloud Firestore.

Your Control

Save Anything is designed around user control. You decide what content you save and how it is organized.

  • Create your own categories
  • Add multiple tags
  • Mark items as favorites
  • Edit saved items
  • Delete saved items permanently
  • Remove uploaded images with their items

The application does not download or store Instagram Reel videos. For Reel items, only the URL is saved.
